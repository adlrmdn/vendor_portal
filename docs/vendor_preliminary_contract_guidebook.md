## 1. Introduction: Understanding the Preliminary Contract

Welcome to the **PT Mega Perintis Tbk Vendor Portal**. 

A **Preliminary Contract (PC)** is an advance manufacturing commitment issued by PT Mega Perintis Tbk to reserve production capacity, yarn procurement, and weaving/knitting lead-times with your mill.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Key Manufacturing Rule**:  
> A Preliminary Contract authorizes **greige and yarn procurement/booking**.  
> **Bulk dyeing and finishing must strictly await the formal Purchase Order (PO)** containing finalized color breakdowns and approved lab dips/shadebands.

## 4. How to Review a Preliminary Contract

Click on any contract number (e.g. `MPG/PC/PDV/26/X/451`) to open the **Contract Review Workspace**:

### 1. Commercial Header
- **Contract Number & Date**: Formal agreement reference.
- **Season / Brand Collection**: e.g. `FALL'26`, `FESTIVE'26`.
- **Currency & Payment Terms**: Confirmed currency (USD, CNY, EUR, IDR) and credit terms (LC, TT, CBD).
- **Incoterm & Mode of Transport**: e.g., `FOB Shanghai — Sea Freight` or `CIF Jakarta`.

### 2. Fabric Technical Specifications Table
Review each contracted item carefully:
- **Item Code & Construction**: Fabric yarn counts, weave structure, and technical description.
- **HS Code**: International 8-digit customs classification (must match your export declaration).
- **Color / Batch Name**: Lab dip reference or seasonal color designation.
- **Cuttable Width & Finished Weight**: Minimum usable width (e.g. `57/58"`) and GSM density.
- **Booking Volume & Price**: Quantity in Yards/Meters and agreed unit price.
- **Under/Overdelivery Tolerance**: Standard production tolerance limits (e.g. `+/- 5.00%`).

### 3. Fabric Delivery Schedule Milestones
- Inspect the milestone delivery splits.
- Verify whether the mill can meet the target FOB/CIF ex-factory readiness dates.

### 4. Standard Clauses & Mill Obligations
Pay careful attention to mandatory quality protocols:
- **Clause 1 & 2**: Yarn booking only. Do not dye bulk fabric without PO in hand.
- **Clause 3**: Pre-shipment documentation mandatory checklist:
  - *Internal Mill Fabric Test Report* (Tensile, tearing, colorfastness, shrinkage).
  - *Shadeband / Shadelot Submissions*.
  - *100% Internal Fabric Inspection Report* (4-point system).
- **Clause 4**: Mandatory SGS or designated third-party inspection at the factory before export dispatch.
- **Clause 5 & 6**: Accuracy of declared HS Codes and compliance with Indonesian import regulations.

## 5. Taking Action on a Contract

At the bottom of the contract review page, you have two primary options:

```
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------+
|         [ Accept Contract ]       |          [ Query Contract ]       |
|    Confirms booking & timelines   |   Requests adjustments/alignment  |
+-----------------------------------+-----------------------------------+
```

### Option A: Accept Contract (Capacity Confirmed)
1. Verify that all specifications, prices, and delivery dates are fully acceptable.
2. Click the green **"Accept Contract"** button.
3. Confirm the confirmation prompt.
4. **What Happens Next**:
   - The contract transitions immediately to **"Confirmed"**.
   - The system appends a digital confirmation record with your user name, timestamp, and IP address.
   - The official contract PDF is permanently sealed with the vendor acceptance stamp.
   - PT Mega Perintis sourcing team is notified to allocate style cutting and issue final Purchase Orders.

### Option B: Query Contract (Request Adjustment)
If your mill identifies any technical discrepancy, MOQ conflict, raw material delay, or pricing misalignment:
1. Click the amber **"Query Contract"** button.
2. A query dialog will appear.
3. **Enter Clear, Professional Remarks**:
   - State the specific line item or schedule date in question.
   - Example: *"Line 1 100% Cotton Twill: Raw yarn delivery delayed due to mill maintenance. Earliest FOB readiness date is 15 Dec 2026 instead of 30 Nov 2026. Please confirm if acceptable."*
4. Click **"Submit Query"**.
5. **What Happens Next**:
   - The contract transitions to **"Vendor Queried"**.
   - PT Mega Perintis buyers are alerted to review your notes.
   - If agreed, the buyer will issue a formal revised contract (`REV`) for your acceptance.

## 7. Transition from Preliminary Contract to Purchase Order (PO)

Once bulk garment styles and color breakdowns are finalized:
1. PT Mega Perintis will issue specific **Purchase Orders (POs)** referencing your Preliminary Contract number.
2. You will receive PO notifications in the **"Purchase Orders"** section of the portal.
3. Production of finished, dyed, and finished fabric proceeds strictly against confirmed PO quantities.
